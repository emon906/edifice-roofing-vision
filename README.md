# KGBuild Cinematic Build

Create a premium, fully responsive cinematic marketing website for KGBuild, a commercial roofing company based in Phoenix, AZ. Business contact: +1 323-696-5188, kgb.roofing@gmail.com. Services: TPO, EPDM, PVC roofing. Service areas: Arizona, Nevada, California, Utah, Texas, and out of state. Credentials: Licensed & Insured, Government Projects.

Make this feel like an expensive US commercial construction brand, not a generic roofing template: dark charcoal/black architectural palette, restrained warm amber highlights, oversized bold editorial typography, cinematic roofing/construction imagery, sharp geometric composition, subtle glass surfaces, premium micro-interactions. Avoid generic rounded-card / SaaS visual language. Use generous whitespace and strong hierarchy.

Build these sections:
1. Full-screen hero with cinematic commercial-roof visual/video-style treatment and dark overlay. Exact headline: “BUILT ABOVE. BUILT TO LAST.” Subtext: “Commercial roofing systems engineered for performance.” CTAs: “Request a Quote” and “Explore Our Work”.
2. KGBuild introduction with impressive animated statistics.
3. Roofing systems: TPO, EPDM, PVC.
4. Full-width cinematic video-style break.
5. Commercial projects showcase.
6. Government & large-scale projects.
7. Why KGBuild.
8. Service-area map-style section covering AZ, NV, CA, UT, TX + nationwide/out-of-state capability.
9. Video/project gallery, featuring these KGBuild Facebook Reels prominently as linked/embed-style project entries wherever embedding is possible: https://www.facebook.com/reel/1373180294396280 ; https://www.facebook.com/reel/1387079893559715 ; https://www.facebook.com/reel/1073439835180749
10. Testimonials.
11. Large premium quote/contact section.
12. Footer with contact details.

Use select subtle muted looping roofing/construction motion-background treatments where feasible and performant. Add sticky initially transparent navbar, cinematic scroll reveals, image zoom effects, smooth section transitions, animated counters, and refined hover behavior. Ensure all navigation and CTAs work, including quote/contact anchoring and phone/email links. Do not fabricate project facts such as years, square footage, or testimonial identities: where figures or testimonials are needed, frame them as visual placeholders/content designed for easy replacement or use non-factual operational messaging. Make the site exceptionally polished on desktop and mobile.
